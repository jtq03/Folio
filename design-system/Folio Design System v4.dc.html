23 · Dark mode

All three surfaces, one set of tokens

Dark mode follows the device setting in the teen app, portal and adult view. Surfaces swap to deep greens instead of black, and lime becomes the primary action. Status colours keep the same meaning with darker backgrounds and lighter text.
